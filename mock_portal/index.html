Guidelines for use of RTI Online Portal

Please read the instructions carefully before proceeding to file an online RTI application.

1. This Web Portal can be used by Indian Citizens to file RTI applications and First Appeals online under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

2. An applicant who desires to obtain information under the RTI Act can pay the prescribed fee through Internet Banking / Credit Card / Debit Card / UPI.

3. The fee for seeking information under Section 6(1) of RTI Act 2005 is Rs. 10/- per application.

4. Below Poverty Line (BPL) Exemption: No application fee is required to be paid by a citizen who belongs to Below Poverty Line (BPL) category under Section 7(5) of the Act. However, the applicant must attach a valid supporting document / BPL certificate proof.

5. Character & Formatting Restriction: Text for RTI request application is limited to 3,000 characters. Only alphabets A-Z, a-z, numbers 0-9 and special characters , . - _ ( ) / @ : & ? \ % and newlines are allowed.

6. Supporting Document: Supporting document should be in PDF format only and file size should not exceed 1 MB.

7. Section 6(2) Protection: Applicant is not required to give reasons for seeking information or any personal details except those necessary for contact.

Please check the guidelines confirmation box before proceeding.
